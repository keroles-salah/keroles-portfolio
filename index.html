About Me

A passionate learner & educator

Data, interfaces, and teaching other people to solve problems.

I'm a Computer Science student at Assiut National University with a strong pull towards Machine Learning, data science and education. I like problems that need both logic and judgement — the ones where the model is only as good as the question you asked it.

As an ITIDA/NTI-certified ML practitioner I've gone from zero to building and deploying real models: a bank churn predictor at 86% accuracy, and a real-time face recognition pipeline. In parallel I've spent two seasons mentoring 15+ students in the ANU and EELU ICPC communities, coach problem solving, and keep a 655-problem competitive programming record myself.

Teaching is the other half of it. I publish Arabic tutorials on SQL, Microsoft Access and data structures, because explaining a concept properly is the only real proof you understand it. I'm currently looking for junior ML / data and web roles where I can keep building things people actually use.
